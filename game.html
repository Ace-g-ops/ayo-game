How to Play Ayo Olopon

🎯 Objective

  • Capture more seeds than your opponent
  • First to 24 points wins, or highest score when board is empty

🎮 Controls

  • Click on any pit on your side to pick up seeds
  • Seeds are distributed counter-clockwise
  • Player 1 controls the bottom row (pits 0-5)
  • Player 2 controls the top row (pits 6-10)

📜 Rules

  • Each pit starts with 4 seeds
  • Distribute seeds one per pit, skipping your starting pit
  • Capture 2-3 seeds from opponent's pit when your last seed lands there
  • Continue capturing backward if previous pits also have 2-3 seeds
  • Game ends when one side has no seeds left

🏆 Winning

  • First player to reach 24 points wins immediately
  • If board empties, player with highest score wins
